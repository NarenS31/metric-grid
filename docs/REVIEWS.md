# External reviews

Three reviews, one per reviewer type. Fill in after each review. **Never claim endorsement.** Quote a reviewer only with their permission, in their own words, and say what you did (or chose not to do) about each point. Keep reviewers' names out unless they agree; use their role.

## How to run a review (15 to 20 minutes each)

1. Show the app for 2 minutes without explaining it (`make demo`), then let them use it.
2. Ask the questions for their type (below). Write down their answers as they say them.
3. Ask: "What is the first thing you would change?" and "What did you think the page was claiming that it is not?"
4. Make changes, then record each one with the commit that made it.
